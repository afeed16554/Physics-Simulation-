Tartini Presets & Illusions

Tartini Tones (Difference Tones) are auditory illusions generated physically inside your inner ear when listening to two loud, high frequencies. Turn up the volume slightly and use these presets to hear a phantom 3rd bass note that doesn't actually exist!

The Physics of Beats

Why do we hear a "beat" above 10 Hz?

Many textbooks state humans only hear beats when the frequency difference (Δf) is less than 10 Hz to 15 Hz. This is an oversimplification! This limit only applies to our brain's ability to count distinct, separate pulses (the "wah-wah" sound).

  • 15 Hz to 50 Hz (Roughness): The pulses happen too fast to count. Your brain interprets this rapid fluttering as a harsh, buzzing, or "rough" sound texture.
  • Above 50 Hz (Tartini Tones): The physical sound waves continue to interfere. The non-linear mechanical properties of your inner ear distort the sound, generating a continuous phantom pitch equal to $f_1 - f_2$. You hear a completely new musical note!
